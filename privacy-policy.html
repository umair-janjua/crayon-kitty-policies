Privacy Policy

App: Crayon Kitty Package ID: com.cubicles.crayonkitty Last Updated: November 15, 2026

The Short Version: Crayon Kitty is a drawing, tracing, and colouring app made for young children and their families. We built it so that nothing about your child leaves the device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

1. Zero Data Collection

  • We do not collect, store, send, or sell any personal or device information.
  • There are no user accounts, no advertisements, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app does not connect to the internet (network permissions are removed in production builds).
  • Everything your child creates stays exclusively on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Crayon Kitty only requests permissions needed for core drawing and tracing features on your device:

Feature / Permission What Happens on Your Device
Camera
android.permission.CAMERA		 Used solely in Camera Trace (AR Trace) mode to show a live view of your paper so a picture can be traced with a real pencil. The camera feed is processed locally in real time and is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere. Snapshots and time-lapse videos are saved only when your child taps the save/record button, and only inside the app's private storage on the device. Videos are recorded without sound; the microphone is never requested or used.
Photos / Media Picker If you choose "My Photo", the system photo picker lets you select a single photo to convert into a tracing outline on your device. The app cannot browse or access the rest of your photo library, and your selected photo never leaves the device.
Drawings & Creations All coloured pictures, drawings, snapshots, and silent time-lapse clips are stored locally in the app's private sandboxed directory on your device. They are permanently removed when you delete them in "My Art" or uninstall the app.
Favourites & Settings Small app preferences (such as favourited templates and whether the welcome intro was completed) are stored locally on the device only.

3. Parental Gate & Sharing

Sharing a drawing outside the app is protected by a parental gate (a verification prompt designed for adults). Only after a grown-up passes the parental gate does the app open your device's built-in system share sheet. The app itself never uploads or transmits files to any external server.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K & Google Play Families Policy)

Because Crayon Kitty collects no personal information or device identifiers and operates completely offline, it is designed to comply with:

  • The U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • The EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Age Appropriate Design Code
  • Google Play's Families Policy and Designed for Families requirements
  • Apple's Kids Category guidelines

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the "Last Updated" date above before releasing any new version of the application.
